## 2024-05-22 - Dependency Hell & Middleware Traps
**Learning:** The project's `requirements.txt` specified `Django>=6.0` which conflicts with `django-celery-beat`'s requirement of `<6.0`. This prevents `pip install` from succeeding. Also, `SecurityTrackingMiddleware` combined with `django-redis` causes `ConnectionError` in tests if Redis isn't available, requiring explicit `override_settings` in tests. `ProductListView` had massive N+1 issues (100+ queries for 20 items) due to missing `prefetch_related` and annotations for fields accessed in the serializer.
**Action:** Always verify `requirements.txt` compatibility early. Use `CaptureQueriesContext` to identify N+1 issues. When writing performance tests, ensure middleware dependencies (like Redis) are mocked or disabled.
